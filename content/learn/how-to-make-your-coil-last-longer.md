A coil is a wearable part. How long it lasts depends on how you use it, but a few habits make a real difference.

## Prime it properly

Before the first use, put a few drops of e-liquid directly onto the coil's cotton and let it soak for a few minutes. A dry coil burns quickly and tastes bad.

## Match the liquid to the coil

Thick, high-VG liquids and very sweet flavours leave more residue on a coil. Use liquids and power settings recommended for your device.

## Take shorter, gentler puffs

Chain vaping keeps the coil hot and does not give the cotton time to re-wet. Pause between puffs.

## Keep the tank topped up

Running a tank close to empty starves the coil. Refill before it gets low.

## Know when to replace it

If you notice a burnt taste, weak flavour, gurgling or leaking, it is usually time for a new coil.
