Nicotine strength is shown on the label in **mg/ml** or as a **percentage**. The two describe the same thing: 20 mg/ml is 2%.

## Freebase vs nicotine salts

- **Freebase nicotine** is usually sold in lower strengths and suits larger devices and bigger clouds.
- **Nicotine salts** are usually sold in higher strengths and feel smoother, and are common in small pod devices and disposables.

## Picking a level

A common approach is to match the strength to your usage and device: smaller, low-power devices generally pair with higher-strength salts, while larger devices generally pair with lower-strength freebase liquid. If you are unsure, ask our team or check the manufacturer's guidance.

## Worth remembering

- Nicotine is addictive.
- Zero-nicotine liquids are available if you prefer flavour only.
- Products are for adults of legal age only.
