Both styles do the same job. The difference is convenience, cost over time and control.

## Disposables

- Ready to use straight away.
- Nothing to refill, charge on most models, or maintain.
- Fixed flavour and strength until the device is finished.

## Refillable devices

- Reusable battery, replaceable coils or pods.
- Wider choice of flavours and strengths, since you buy the liquid separately.
- More set-up and upkeep, but usually lower running costs.

## Which should you choose?

Choose a disposable if you want the simplest option. Choose a refillable system if you want more flavour choice and are happy to look after a device.
