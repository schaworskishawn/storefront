E-liquid keeps best when it is protected from the things that degrade it.

## Keep it cool and dark

Heat and direct sunlight can change the flavour and darken the liquid. A drawer or cupboard is better than a car dashboard or windowsill.

## Keep the cap tight

Air exposure dulls flavour over time. Close the bottle firmly after each fill.

## Keep it safe

Store bottles upright, away from children and pets, and clearly labelled.

## Check before you use it

If a liquid smells or tastes off, looks unusually dark or separated, do not use it.
