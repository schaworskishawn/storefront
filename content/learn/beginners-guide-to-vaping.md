Vaping devices heat a liquid (e-liquid) into a vapour you inhale. Getting the basics right makes the whole experience simpler and more enjoyable.

## The main parts of a device

- **Battery** – powers the device. Disposables have it built in; refillable devices use a rechargeable one.
- **Coil** – the heating element that warms the e-liquid. Coils wear out over time and need replacing.
- **Pod or tank** – holds the e-liquid.
- **Mouthpiece** – where you inhale.

## Choosing a device type

1. **Disposables** – ready to use out of the box, nothing to refill or charge on most models.
2. **Pod systems** – compact and simple, with replaceable pods or refillable cartridges.
3. **Mods and tanks** – more control and customisation, aimed at experienced users.

## Understanding e-liquid

E-liquid is mostly propylene glycol (PG) and vegetable glycerin (VG), plus flavouring and, optionally, nicotine. A higher PG ratio tends to give a stronger throat feel; a higher VG ratio gives thicker vapour.

## Tips for a good start

- Start with a device type that matches how you plan to use it.
- Read the strength and ingredients on the label before you buy.
- Keep devices and liquids out of reach of children and pets.
- You must be of legal age to buy and use vaping products.
