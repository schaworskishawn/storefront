# Talent recruiting support

## Use this when

- A role has approved criteria and candidate evidence to organize.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Role (required)
- Approved criteria (required)
- Candidate evidence

## Procedure

1. Read the criteria and refuse any that concern a protected characteristic.
2. Summarize the role from the approved requirements.
3. Map each candidate's supplied evidence to each criterion.
4. Write interview questions tied to the criteria.
5. Mark criteria for which no evidence was supplied.

## What you produce

- A stored HiringPacket record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- No criterion or wording concerns a protected characteristic.
- Every statement about a candidate comes from supplied evidence.
- No ranking is presented as a hiring decision.

## When something goes wrong

- If a criterion concerns a protected characteristic, stop before any drafting.

## Example

**Request:** "Talent: prepare hiring packet."

**Result:** A hiring packet draft is stored with a role summary, a criterion-by-criterion evidence map for each candidate, the gaps, and interview questions, waiting for a person's review.

## Limits

- It makes no hiring decision and ranks nobody. It works only from the criteria and evidence supplied, and refuses criteria that refer to a protected characteristic.
- Prepares materials. Makes no hiring decision, infers no protected trait, sends no invitation.
- The wording comes from a model and is checked by code. A person reviews every draft before it is used.
