# Safeguard workplace records

## Use this when

- A period's safety and waste records need reviewing for gaps.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Period (YYYY-MM) (required)
- Inspections required in the period
- Inspections recorded
- Incidents and spills recorded
- Waste movements

## Procedure

1. Read the inspections, incidents and waste movements for the period.
2. Count inspections against the number required.
3. List incidents with no completed follow-up.
4. Reconcile waste generated against waste removed, by stream.
5. Name the responsible person for each gap where supplied.

## What you produce

- A stored WorkplaceWasteReview record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- Counts are against the stated requirement.
- Waste is reconciled per stream, never across streams.
- No instruction for a physical emergency response is written.

## When something goes wrong

- If the required number of inspections wasn't supplied, the count is shown without a verdict.

## Example

**Request:** "Safeguard: review workplace and waste records."

**Result:** A workplace and waste review is stored with inspection counts against requirements, incidents needing follow-up, a balance per waste stream, undocumented collections, and drafted follow-ups.

## Limits

- It reviews records. It writes no emergency or spill response, certifies nothing as safe, and authorizes no disposal.
- Reviews records. Issues no safety certificate, disposal permission or emergency instruction.
- The figures and findings are calculated by code from what is supplied. No model writes them.
