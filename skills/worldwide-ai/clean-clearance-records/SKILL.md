# Clean clearance records

## Use this when

- A cleaning or changeover record needs comparing with its procedure.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Line or equipment (required)
- Approved procedure and version (required)
- Previous product and label (required)
- Next product and label (required)
- Procedure steps (required)
- Recorded observations

## Procedure

1. Read the procedure's steps and the record.
2. For each step, find who performed it, who verified it, and when.
3. Flag steps missing a performer, a verifier or a time.
4. Flag a step verified by the person who performed it, where that isn't allowed.
5. List the gaps.

## What you produce

- A stored ClearanceReview record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- Every procedure step is accounted for or listed as a gap.
- A completed record is not treated as proof the line is clean.
- Nothing is attested.

## When something goes wrong

- If the record refers to a different procedure version, say so before comparing.

## Example

**Request:** "Clean: prepare line-clearance review."

**Result:** A clearance review is stored with each procedure step's recorded performer and verifier, the steps still outstanding, and steps whose verification isn't independent.

## Limits

- It compares records to the procedure. It does not attest that any physical cleaning or clearance happened: only a recorded observation by an authorized person does that.
- Compares records to the procedure. Never attests that physical cleaning or clearance occurred.
- The figures and findings are calculated by code from what is supplied. No model writes them.
