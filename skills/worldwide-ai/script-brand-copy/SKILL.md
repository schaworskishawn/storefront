# Script brand copy

## Use this when

- Copy fields need writing or revising from verified facts.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- What the copy is for (required)
- Fields to write (required)
- Content brief (required)
- Verified facts (required)
- Brand voice
- Current copy

## Procedure

1. Read the brief and the verified facts.
2. Write each requested field in the brand voice.
3. Keep every specification exactly as supplied.
4. Leave out any claim the facts don't support.
5. List what was left out and why.

## What you produce

- A stored CopyDraft record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- No specification differs from the supplied facts.
- No health, cessation or safety claim appears.
- No keyword stuffing.

## When something goes wrong

- If a requested field can't be written from the facts, return it empty with the reason.

## Example

**Request:** "Script: draft approved brand copy."

**Result:** A copy draft is stored with each requested field's text and the facts it rests on, and the claims that were omitted and why, waiting for a person's review.

## Limits

- It writes from supplied facts only: no invented specification, health claim or promise. Nothing is published.
- Writes from supplied facts only. No invented specifications, health claims or promises.
- The wording comes from a model and is checked by code. A person reviews every draft before it is used.
