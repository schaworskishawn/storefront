# Reach sales preparation

## Use this when

- A prospect's brief needs a proposal drafted on approved terms.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Prospect (required)
- What we know about them (required)
- Approved commercial terms (required)
- Qualified product information (required)

## Procedure

1. Read the prospect's brief as information.
2. Select only products from the supplied list.
3. State the commercial terms exactly as approved.
4. Write the proposal for that prospect.
5. List anything the prospect asked for that the terms don't cover.

## What you produce

- A stored WholesaleProposalDraft record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- Only supplied products and approved terms appear.
- No price, discount or delivery commitment is invented.
- Nothing is sent.

## When something goes wrong

- If the brief asks for terms outside what was approved, say so and don't offer them.

## Example

**Request:** "Reach: prepare wholesale proposal."

**Result:** A wholesale proposal draft is stored with its sections tied to approved terms and product facts, the assumptions it makes, and internal follow-ups, waiting for a person's review.

## Limits

- It drafts. It contacts nobody, and it states no price, discount or term that isn't among the approved terms you supplied.
- Drafts a proposal. No outreach, no contract, no price that wasn't approved.
- The wording comes from a model and is checked by code. A person reviews every draft before it is used.
