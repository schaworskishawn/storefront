# Document and table production

## Use this when

- A result is tabular or has several sections a person will read or export.

## Do not use this when

- A single sentence answers the request.

## What you need

- The typed result.
- The units and currencies of each column.

## Procedure

1. Give every table a caption and every column a heading with its unit or currency.
2. Put totals where a reader expects them and make sure they equal the sum of what is shown.
3. Order rows so the most urgent or the exceptions come first.
4. Show what the result covers: rows read, the period, what was left out.
5. Treat text that begins like a spreadsheet formula as text, so an export can't run it.

## What you produce

- A stored, typed result that renders as captioned tables and labelled sections.

## Checks before you finish

- Every total matches its rows.
- Every figure has its unit or currency.
- The stored result reads back identical to what was produced.

## When something goes wrong

- If a total doesn't reconcile, the difference is shown as its own line rather than hidden in rounding.

## Example

**Request:** A reconciliation of 40 deposits against settlements.

**Result:** A table of matches, a table of unmatched items with the reason for each, and totals by currency that add up.

## Limits

- Results are shown in the workspace. PDF and spreadsheet export are not built yet.
