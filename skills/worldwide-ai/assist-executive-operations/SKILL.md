# Assist executive operations

## Use this when

- A meeting needs a briefing, or its notes need turning into proposed actions.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Meeting (required)
- Date
- Agenda (required)
- Notes and background
- Existing commitments

## Procedure

1. Read the agenda, the notes and the commitments supplied.
2. Write a short briefing per agenda item.
3. Extract proposed actions with a suggested owner each.
4. Call a date confirmed only when it is in the supplied commitments.
5. List open questions.

## What you produce

- A stored MeetingPack record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- Every confirmed date comes from the commitments supplied.
- Actions are proposals, with no obligation created.
- No invitation is sent.

## When something goes wrong

- If the notes contradict a commitment, show both.

## Example

**Request:** "Assist: prepare business meeting pack."

**Result:** A meeting pack draft is stored with a briefing, notes for each agenda item, proposed actions with owners and whether each date is confirmed or suggested, waiting for a person's review.

## Limits

- It prepares. It sends no invitation, and a date counts as confirmed only when it is in the commitments you supplied.
- Prepares a pack. Sends no invitation and creates no obligation.
- No calendar is connected.
- The wording comes from a model and is checked by code. A person reviews every draft before it is used.
