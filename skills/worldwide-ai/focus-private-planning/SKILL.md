# Focus private planning

## Use this when

- A person wants their own goals broken into next actions.

## Do not use this when

- What the function needs has not been supplied: it reports what is missing and creates nothing, so gather it first.
- The request belongs to another agent's function: it goes through Nexus, not through this procedure.
- The request is to act outside the workspace (send, post, pay, file, or change a live record): this procedure only prepares and stores a result.

## What you need

- Plan for (required)
- Goals (required)
- Fixed commitments
- Preferences

## Procedure

1. Use only what the person shared for this request.
2. Break each goal into the next concrete action.
3. Fit the actions around the commitments they listed.
4. Keep the plan short enough to act on.
5. Store it in the person's own scope.

## What you produce

- A stored PrivatePersonalPlan record, checked against its declared format before it is kept.
- It waits for a person to accept or reject it.
- Its sources, anything missing, assumptions and warnings are listed with it.

## Checks before you finish

- The plan is visible to that person only and appears in no shared list, count or report.
- No business record is read.
- Nothing the person didn't share is assumed about them.

## When something goes wrong

- If the goals conflict with the commitments, show the conflict and let the person choose.

## Example

**Request:** "Focus: plan my personal priorities."

**Result:** A private personal plan is stored in your own scope with a few next actions tied to your goals and timing suggestions, visible only to you.

## Limits

- It stays yours: the task and the plan never appear in shared briefings, team views, reports or another person's lists.
- Private to the person who asked. Never feeds shared briefings, team views or business reports.
- The wording comes from a model and is checked by code. A person reviews every draft before it is used.
